# Sources — Tuesday 24 November 2026

Every story on the poster was researched live on the morning of Tuesday
24 November 2026 (WIB) and cross-checked across at least two independent
outlets where possible. Stories resting on a single outlet or a single
roundup are labelled with that outlet on the poster itself. Anything whose
event belonged to an earlier day was kept off the page and is named in the
DATE CHECK box instead.

## Method note

Region counts on the poster count each story once, by its primary dateline
or subject: Europe 4, Middle East 2, Americas 4, Asia-Pacific 12, Africa 1,
Oceania 1, World 1 — 25 in total. The stat band is drawn from the same
stories. The DATE CHECK box lists the older items deliberately excluded:
the ICJ ruling itself (Thu), the Senegal migrant boat sinking (Wed),
Zanzibar's six-hotel fire (Sat) and Waymo's freeway ban (11 Nov).
